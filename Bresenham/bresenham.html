Bresenham's Line Algorithm by Mihailo Despotovic, February 2011

Which pixels should light up to draw a straight line? Drag the ends of the line, or click to move the nearer one. My 2011 version solves the basic case, lines going right and rising at most 45°, tracking the error between the true line (red) and the pixels with a floating-point number. The other seven directions are mirror images of it: swap the ends, flip it upside down, or swap x and y, draw it, and mirror the pixels back. Compare it as written, with the mirroring, and as the integer-only version that needs no mirroring.
