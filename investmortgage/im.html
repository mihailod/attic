Mortgage or Invest? by Mihailo Despotovic, May 2008

You have enough savings to buy a house outright. Is it better to pay cash, or to take a mortgage for the same amount and invest the savings? With the mortgage, the portfolio makes the payments and gets back the tax saved by deducting the interest; whatever is left once the mortgage is paid off is what investing gained.
