Permutations by Insertion by Mihailo Despotovic, May 2009

Every ordering of the letters of a word. Take the permutations of all but the last letter, and insert the last letter into every position of each one; the same step, one letter at a time, builds them all. A word of n letters has n! = 1 × 2 × … × n of them.
