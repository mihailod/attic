The Birthday Paradox by Mihailo Despotovic, September 2005

How many people must be in a room before two of them probably share a birthday? Surprisingly few: with 23 people the chance is already better than even. The chance that everyone's birthday differs is 365/365 × 364/365 × 363/365 × …, one factor per person, and the chance of a shared birthday is 1 minus that.
