Dining Philosophers by Mihailo Despotovic, August 2005

Dining philosophers sit around a table with a fork between each pair of neighbours. Each one thinks, gets hungry, picks up the forks on both sides, eats and puts them back. If they all pick up the left fork first, they can end up each holding one fork and waiting forever for the other. My solution: the odd-numbered philosophers pick up the right fork first. Switch to the naive way and click “Everyone gets hungry at once”: all the forks go back on the table and everyone reaches for one at the same moment.
