ChuChu Cheese V1.0 by Mihailo Despotovic, October 2003

My version of the Puzzle mode of Sega's ChuChu Rocket! for the Dreamcast, with a cheese instead of the rocket. Put your arrows on the board, then press play: the mice and cats run along, turn when they hit a wall and follow the arrows. Get every mouse into the cheese, without letting one fall into a hole, a cat reach the cheese or a cat catch a mouse.

Click an arrow in the tray, then a square on the board to put it there; click a placed arrow to take it back. Arrows can't go on the cheese or a hole. ▶ plays, ▶▶ plays four times as fast, ■ stops and puts everyone back. The four levels are my level files from 2003, which were really tests of the game rather than finished puzzles; level 1 has one cat fewer than in 2003, so that it can be solved.

Pick a tool and a direction, then click the board: mice and cats face the chosen direction, and a wall goes on that side of the square. With Arrow chosen, click the tray below the board to give the level an arrow of that direction (up to four). Erase, or a right-click, removes what is on a square, or one of its walls per click, or the last arrow in the tray. Levels are saved in the 2003 file format.
