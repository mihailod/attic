TeekoTeacher Instructions

1. Rules of Teeko

The game is played on a five by five chess-like board. Each player has four equal pieces.

The first four moves define the opening phase. During this phase, each player alternatively puts one piece on an unoccupied field on the board. Then, the pieces are treated as kings in Chess — they can be moved only to unoccupied adjacent fields.

The goal of the game is to make a four in a row, diagonal or square formation. The game is considered to be drawn if the same pair of moves (move = ply + reply) gets repeated three times in a row.

2. How to play

During the opening phase, just click on the field you want to play. During the post-opening phase, two clicks are required to move a piece. First, click on the field occupied by the piece you want to move. Then click on the field you want to move that piece to.

3. How to learn

TeekoTeacher allows you to play on behalf both sides and to learn from advices provided by the program. You can choose to receive advices for the first player, for the second player and for both players.

During the game, depending on the setup and the availability of the current position in the database, TeekoTeacher might provide an advice for the next best move (where "the best" is "the best according to the database.") This advice will be represented as a flashing field (in the opening phase) and as a flashing pair of board fields ('from' and 'to' fields) during the post-opening phase. You can follow those advices and try to learn from them or, alternatively, you can choose to ignore them.

You can also use the log area to copy and then save game logs so you can study them later at your convenience.
